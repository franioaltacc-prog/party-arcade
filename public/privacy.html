Privacy Policy 🔒

What Party Arcade knows about you, why, and what you can do about it. In plain English.

📅 Last updated: 11 October 2026

⚡ The short version

  • 👤An account is just a username and password. No email, real name, age, phone number or location.
  • 🔐Your password is stored as a secure scramble (hash), so nobody can read it, not even me.
  • 🏆Your profile, stats and scores are public: anyone can see them on your profile page and on the leaderboards. Don’t put personal info in your username, bio or chat.
  • 🚫No ads, no selling your data, no tracking or analytics cookies, and no real money (casino coins are fake).
  • 🗑️You can delete your account any time in your account settings. It removes your stats too.

🧑‍💻 Who runs Party Arcade

Party Arcade is a free hobby project run by one person (me) in Poland. Because I’m in the EU, the EU’s data protection law, the GDPR, applies. In GDPR words, I’m the “controller” of your data.

Questions about privacy, or want to use your rights? Contact me here: [MY CONTACT EMAIL OR FORM]

📦 What data I collect

If you make an account

  • Username and password. That’s all you need. I never ask for your email, real name, age, phone number or location.
  • Your password is saved as a secure hash (a one-way scramble made with scrypt), never as plain text. Even I can’t see it.
  • The emoji avatar and colour you pick, and the date you joined.

Game data (logged-in players)

  • Your XP and level, stats for each game (like games played, wins and best scores), your match history (your last 60 games), solo high scores and the achievements you’ve earned.
  • Your bio, if you write one.
  • The rewards you choose to wear: an aura, an animated avatar and a Slope ball skin (you unlock them by levelling up and earning achievements).
  • Your online status: whether you’re online right now, which game you’re playing, and when you were last online.

If you play as a guest

  • A nickname, emoji avatar and colour. The server only uses them while you’re connected, so other players can see who’s who.
  • Guest stats aren’t saved on the server. Your browser remembers your guest look (and some solo best scores) on your own device. See Browser storage.

Chat messages

  • You can chat in the lobby chat on the home page and in room chat inside games. Other people in the same chat can read what you write.

Technical data

  • Your IP address and basic connection info are used by the game server and my hosting company to run the site and to stop abuse. For example, the server limits how many accounts and login attempts can come from one address. The site doesn’t save IP addresses in its database. It only keeps these counts in the server’s memory for up to an hour.
  • My hosting company (Render) may keep its own server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a limited time under its own rules.
  • When the server restarts, any open rooms (the room code, which game it is, and the temporary player IDs of the host and anyone who was kicked) are saved for up to 10 minutes, so everyone can jump back in.

🍪 Browser storage (no tracking!)

Party Arcade doesn’t use cookies. It saves a few small things in your browser’s own storage (called localStorage and sessionStorage) so the site works the way you left it. None of it is used for tracking or ads, and it isn’t shared with anyone.

WhatWhy

You can delete all of this any time by clearing your browser’s site data for Party Arcade.

🤝 Other companies that handle data

I don’t sell or give your data to anyone. But a few services help run the site:

  • Render hosts the website and game server, so everything you send to the site passes through it.
  • Turso runs the database where accounts, stats and match history are stored.

These services may store or process data outside Poland and the EU.

Fonts, animations and pictures from other sites

To look good, the site loads some files straight from other websites. When your browser downloads them, those sites can see your IP address and basic browser info (like any website you visit):

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) for the Bungee and Fredoka fonts.
  • cdnjs (by Cloudflare) for the GSAP animation library, and for three.js in Slope.
  • jsDelivr for the 3D emoji pictures (Microsoft’s Fluent Emoji).

👀 What’s public

  • Profile pages are public. Anyone can see your username, avatar (with any aura or animation you wear), level, stats, achievements, recent games, bio, when you joined, and whether you’re online (and what you’re playing) or when you were last online.
  • Leaderboards show usernames and scores, and the Find players page lets anyone search for players who have accounts.
  • In rooms, other players see your name, avatar and colour, and everything you write in chat.

💡 Please don’t put personal info like your real name, school, address or phone number in your username, bio or chat messages.

🛠️ How your data is used

  • To run your account and the games.
  • To save your stats, XP and levels and show the leaderboards.
  • To make chat work.
  • To keep the site fun and safe: moderation, like banning accounts, kicking people from rooms and removing bad content.

As the admin, I can see account info and stats, see who’s online and which rooms they’re in, reset passwords, and ban or delete accounts. I do that only to run and moderate the site. I can’t see your password.

🧒 Kids and age

  • Party Arcade doesn’t ask for your age.
  • In Poland you need to be 16 to agree to this policy yourself. If you’re younger, please get a parent or guardian’s OK before making an account.
  • If you’re a parent and want your child’s account deleted, contact me and I’ll remove it.

✋ Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask me to:

  • See the data I have about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Fix data that’s wrong. You can change your avatar, colour and bio yourself in your account settings.
  • Delete your account and data.
  • Object to how I use your data, or ask me to limit it.

To ask, contact me. I may need to check that the account is really yours first.

🗑️ Delete it yourself

Open your account (top right) → Delete my account, and type your password. This removes your account, stats, XP and match history straight away, and you disappear from the leaderboards and the player search.

🏛️ Complaints

If you think I’m handling your data wrongly, you can complain to the Polish data protection authority, UODO (Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych): uodo.gov.pl. I’d love the chance to fix it first, though!

⏳ How long data is kept

  • Your account and stats are kept until you delete your account, or until it hasn’t been used for [I'LL DECIDE, e.g. 2 years].
  • Deleted accounts are removed from the database and the leaderboards.
  • Match history only keeps your last 60 games.
  • Logins run out after 180 days.
  • Login and sign-up limits (by IP address) are only kept in memory, for up to an hour.

🛡️ Security

  • Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and login keys are stored scrambled too.
  • The site uses HTTPS, so what you send is encrypted on the way.
  • The server runs or checks the online games, so you can’t fake a win just by changing the web page, and logins are limited so passwords can’t be guessed over and over.

But to be honest: no website is 100% secure. Please use a password you don’t use anywhere else.
